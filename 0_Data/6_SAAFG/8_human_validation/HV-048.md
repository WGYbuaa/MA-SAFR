Requirement Description:
The identity verification platform must enable seamless user onboarding by accommodating natural variations in physical appearance, including accessories and hairstyles, during selfie-to-ID matching. The system shall automatically authenticate submitted identity documents and facilitate immediate claim processing upon verification. Payment distribution should support flexible delivery addresses and diverse cash withdrawal methods to maximize user convenience.

- BF1: The user submits a selfie and identity document.
- BF2: The identity verification platform performs selfie-to-ID matching accommodating physical variations.
- BF3: The system evaluates the verification result condition.
- BF4: The system automatically authenticates the submitted identity documents.
- BF5: The system facilitates immediate claim processing.
- BF6: The system determines the payment delivery method.
- BF7: The system distributes payment to the flexible delivery address or cash withdrawal point.
